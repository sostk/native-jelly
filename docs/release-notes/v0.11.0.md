# v0.11.0 — Lossless audio to your receiver, user pictures and Jellyfin preferences

If you have an AV receiver or soundbar, DTS and Dolby TrueHD soundtracks can now reach it unchanged, and audio the television cannot decode no longer makes your server convert it. This release also shows each user's own picture, saves audio and subtitle preferences to your Jellyfin account, and lists every kind of video library.

## Before you choose "Download and install" for HBR: it loads a kernel module as root

This applies only if you choose it: nothing below happens unless you pick **Download and install** under **Settings → Video & playback → Atmos, TrueHD and DTS:X (root)** and then answer **Install**. The row appears only on webOS 6 televisions with **Lossless audio to receiver** on, and it only works on a rooted TV with the Homebrew Channel.

When you choose it, Native Jelly downloads the earc_route kernel module and its loader from this project's GitHub repository (raw.githubusercontent.com) at a fixed version, refuses them unless they match the copy this release was built with, and installs them as root through the Homebrew Channel: the files go to /var/lib/webosbrew/nativejelly-hbr, a Homebrew Channel startup script loads the module at every start, and a device node is added inside Native Jelly's own sandbox. This is the first Native Jelly release that can download anything other than from your own Jellyfin server, and the first that can write outside its own directories.

**What to do:** nothing, unless you want full-rate surround formats on a rooted webOS 6 TV. If you install it, **Remove** in the same place deletes everything it installed and switches the module off at once; it is unloaded at the next restart.

**What this does not change:** the module runs in the television's kernel. It was tested on one LG C1 (webOS 6); on a television it was not built for, its loader refuses to load it and nothing changes, but use it at your own risk.

## Breaking: going back to v0.10.0 or earlier asks you to sign in again

Native Jelly now stores its sign-in under its own name instead of the one upstream PlxNative uses, so the two apps can be installed side by side. Updating moves your sign-in for you. If you later install v0.10.0 or an older version over this one, it finds no sign-in and asks you to sign in again.

## What's new

- **Lossless audio to a receiver.** **Settings → Video & playback → Lossless audio to receiver** sends DTS and Dolby TrueHD soundtracks unchanged to an AV receiver or soundbar, which decodes them. DTS reaches it at least as its 5.1 core, over HDMI ARC, eARC or optical. On webOS 6 over HDMI eARC or ARC, DTS-HD MA also reaches it in full, inside the television's Dolby Digital Plus pass-through; on other firmware TrueHD is still converted to Dolby Digital. It is used only while the TV's sound goes to the receiver, and is checked again at every playback, so the TV's own speakers never get a bitstream. Set **Digital Sound Output** to **Pass Through** in the TV's sound settings. Thanks to [Mokto](https://github.com/Mokto), who wrote it.
- **TrueHD and Atmos, DTS:X and full-rate DTS-HD MA on rooted webOS 6 TVs.** These need the HDMI eARC link's high-bitrate mode, which LG does not open to apps. On a rooted webOS 6 TV, the new **Atmos, TrueHD and DTS:X (root)** setting can install the kernel module that opens it; see the section above before you do. A separate companion app that does the same is in the [repository](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/tree/main/companion/hbr).
- **Audio the TV cannot decode now plays without the server converting it.** TrueHD, FLAC, LPCM and similar tracks are converted to Dolby Digital on the television, so the video and the rest of the file come unchanged from your server.
- **DTS-HD MA 7.1 plays directly** by its 5.1 core on televisions with a DTS decoder, instead of being converted by the server.
- **Each user's own picture** on Who's watching?, Add a user and the profile chip. Thanks to [Mokto](https://github.com/Mokto).
- **Audio & subtitles settings save to your Jellyfin account:** preferred audio language, subtitle mode and language, and whether to play the default audio track, the same preferences Jellyfin's own apps use. Thanks to [aimnnnn](https://github.com/aimnnnn) for the report ([#41](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/issues/41)).
- **Mixed, Home Videos and Music Videos libraries** are listed and open. Mixed libraries used to be missing and the other two opened empty; their listing is now called **All**. Thanks to [watsonmaster](https://github.com/watsonmaster) and [nomedialoaded](https://github.com/nomedialoaded) for the reports ([#35](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/issues/35), [#38](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/issues/38)).
- **Remove from Continue Watching stays removed.** The card is hidden on this television and comes back only when you watch more of it; nothing on the server is changed. Thanks to [Mokto](https://github.com/Mokto).

## Fixed

- **Native Jelly and PlxNative could not both be installed:** whichever was installed second failed to load its saved sign-in. Thanks to [dino-cikatic](https://github.com/dino-cikatic) for the report and fix ([#39](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/issues/39)). See the sign-in note above.
- **Audio could go missing for stretches of a 4K remux** while the video buffer was full; it was dropped instead of waiting.
- **Some Dolby Vision films stayed on Buffering after a resume or seek** on televisions whose Dolby Vision decoder can only start on certain frames. Those films now play their HDR10 picture in that case.
- **The Account menu could say "No tracks" on first launch** while the app's storage helper was still starting. It now says the account is still loading and keeps Settings available ([#51](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/issues/51)).
- **Diagnostics could say "webOS unknown"** on a set whose firmware details file could not be read, which also disabled firmware-specific behaviour; the app now reads the release from a second source.
- **Long titles could end in a broken character** when shortened.

## Compatibility

- **Plays video, verified by watching it — 2021 LG sets.** One television, an LG C1 running webOS 6.5.3, on which this release's lossless audio paths were played to an LG soundbar on eARC, and the HBR install, Continue Watching removal and the Account menu were checked, all with development builds of these changes. Not every fix above was re-checked on it; the technical audit lists which were.
- **Plays video, reported by someone else — 2021 LG sets.** [Mokto](https://github.com/Mokto) reported DTS-HD MA, DTS:X and TrueHD Atmos reaching a Sony STR-DN1080 receiver from an LG C1 (webOS 6) with builds of the lossless-audio change, in November 2026 ([#46](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/pull/46)).
- **Starts, and nothing further is known — every other firmware from webOS 4.0 up.** The release run checks this binary's libraries and symbols against every firmware image webosbrew has from platform release 4.4.2 up, and fails if any of them would refuse to start it; webosbrew has no image of platform releases 4.0 to 4.4.1, so those are expected to start but were not checked; the matrix is in the [technical audit](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.11.0.md). That grades startup and says nothing about whether video plays.

If your set is in the last group, [tell us what happened](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/issues). It working is as useful a report as it failing.

## Known issues

- **Direct play of some large Dolby Vision Profile 8 files with Atmos stops after about 45 seconds**, now reported on an LG C3 (webOS 11.2), an LG SM8500 (webOS 4.10) and an LG C1 (webOS 6.5.3) ([#36](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/issues/36)). Not yet fixed; this release does not change it.
- **A server address with a path after the host name** (for example behind some proxies) is not supported ([#42](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/issues/42)).
- **Playback can fail over HTTPS through a reverse proxy.** Carried from earlier releases; these changes do not address it.
- **Video re-encoding remains H.264**, even when the server is allowed to encode HEVC.

## Help test this release

**If you have an AV receiver or soundbar, turn on Lossless audio to receiver and play a DTS or TrueHD film.** It has been heard on one LG soundbar and one Sony receiver, both from an LG C1. The DTS core route is not limited to webOS 6 but has not been tried on any other firmware, so reports from other receivers, from webOS 4, 5 and 7 or later sets, and from optical connections are what we need. [Open an issue](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/issues) with your TV model and firmware, your receiver, the soundtrack format, and what the receiver's display showed. It working is as useful a report as it failing.

## Installing

Download **com.sostk.nativejelly_0.11.0_arm.ipk** and install it with the [Homebrew Channel](https://github.com/webosbrew/webos-homebrew-channel) or [dev-manager-desktop](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop). You do **not** need a rooted TV.

Nothing in this distribution chain is signed, so this sha256 is what tells you the file you have is the file published here:

```
__IPK_SHA256__  com.sostk.nativejelly_0.11.0_arm.ipk
```

[Installing and checking a download](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/blob/main/docs/install-and-verify.md) covers the other assets, how to check the hash on each platform, and the Developer Mode expiry that uninstalls your apps. This package bundles FFmpeg under LGPL-2.1-or-later and its complete corresponding source is attached to this release. Exactly what was built, verified and shipped is in the [technical audit for v0.11.0](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.11.0.md).
